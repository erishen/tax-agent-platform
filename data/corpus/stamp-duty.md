## 印花税税目与税率 / Stamp Duty Items and Rates
合同类：借款合同 0.05‰、购销合同 0.3‰、加工承揽合同 0.5‰、财产租赁合同 1‰、技术合同 0.3‰。
产权转移书据（股权转让除外）0.5‰；营业账簿中资金账簿按实收资本与资本公积合计 0.25‰。

Contracts: loan 0.05‰, purchase/sale 0.3‰, processing 0.5‰, property lease 1‰, technology 0.3‰.
Property transfer documents (except equity transfer) 0.5‰; capital account books 0.25‰ on paid-in + reserves.

## 纳税人与免税 / Taxpayers and Exemptions
在境内书立应税凭证的单位和个人为纳税人。
农业生产者买卖合同、无息贴息借款合同等免征印花税。

Entities/individuals executing taxable documents in China are taxpayers.
Sales contracts of agricultural producers and interest-free loan contracts are exempt.
