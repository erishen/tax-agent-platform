## 企业所得税税率 / CIT Rates
标准税率 25%。高新技术企业 15%。小型微利企业前 100 万元利润适用 20%。
研发费用可享受 100% 加计扣除（在实际支出基础上再加计 100%）。

Standard rate: 25%. High-tech enterprises: 15%. Small low-profit enterprises: 20% on first 1M CNY.
R&D expenses qualify for 100% super deduction (additional 100% on top of actual expense).

## 税前扣除与亏损结转 / Deductions and Loss Carry-forward
合理的工资薪金、业务招待费（按发生额 60% 扣除且不超过营收 5‰）、广告费（不超过营收 15%）准予扣除。
亏损最长可向后结转 5 年弥补。

Reasonable salaries, business entertainment (60% of amount, capped at 5‰ of revenue), and advertising (15% of revenue) are deductible.
Losses may be carried forward for up to 5 years.
