## 综合所得税率 / Comprehensive Income Tax Rates
居民个人综合所得（工资薪金、劳务报酬、稿酬、特许权使用费）按年合并，适用 3% 至 45% 的七级超额累进税率。
起征点为年度 6 万元（每月 5000 元）。

Comprehensive income of resident individuals is consolidated annually and taxed at a 7-bracket progressive rate from 3% to 45%.
The basic exemption is 60K CNY per year (5K per month).

## 专项附加扣除 / Special Additional Deductions
包括子女教育、继续教育、大病医疗、住房贷款利息、住房租金、赡养老人、3 岁以下婴幼儿照护共七项。
例如每个子女教育每月 2000 元，赡养老人每月 3000 元（独生子女）。

Seven categories: children's education, continuing education, serious illness, housing loan interest, housing rent, elder care, and infant care under 3.
E.g. 2000 CNY/month per child for education, 3000 CNY/month for elder care (only child).
