## 增值税税率变化 / VAT Rate Changes
软件服务适用 6% 增值税税率，自 2024 年 1 月起施行。
跨境数字服务在特定条件下可适用较低税率。
月销售额低于 10 万元人民币的小规模纳税人免征增值税。

Software services are subject to 6% VAT rate effective from January 2024.
Cross-border digital services may qualify for reduced rates under specific conditions.
Small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales below 100K CNY are exempt from VAT.

## 一般纳税人与进项税额 / General Taxpayers and Input Tax
一般纳税人适用 13%（货物）、9%（建筑/不动产/农产品等）、6%（服务）三档税率。
当期进项税额可从销项税额中抵扣，形成应纳税额 = 销项税额 - 进项税额。

General taxpayers apply 13% (goods), 9% (construction/real-estate/agriculture), 6% (services).
Input tax incurred in the current period is deductible from output tax.
