## 增量留抵退税 / Incremental Credit Refund
符合条件的纳税人，当期新增留抵税额可申请退还。
一般行业需满足连续 6 个月（含）增量留抵且第 6 个月增量不低于 50 万元等条件。

Eligible taxpayers may apply to refund the incremental input-tax credit of the current period.
General industries must meet conditions such as 6 consecutive months of incremental credit with the 6th month ≥ 500K CNY.

## 先进制造业 / Advanced Manufacturing
先进制造业、小微等企业可适用更宽松的按月全额退还增量留抵税额政策。

Advanced manufacturing and micro/small enterprises may qualify for monthly full refund of incremental credits under relaxed rules.
