## Federal Corporate Income Tax Rate / 联邦企业所得税税率

The US federal corporate income tax rate is a flat 21% on taxable income,
established by the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) and effective for tax years
beginning after December 31, 2017.

美国联邦企业所得税采用 21% 的单一税率，依据《减税与就业法案》(TCJA)，
自 2018 纳税年度起施行。

## Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax (CAMT) / 企业替代性最低税

A 15% corporate alternative minimum tax applies to applicable corporations
with average annual adjusted financial statement income exceeding USD 1 billion
over a three-year period, effective from 2023.

对三年平均调整后财务报表收入超过 10 亿美元的适用公司，自 2023 年起
征收 15% 的企业替代性最低税 (CAMT)。

## Research Credit / 研发税收抵免

Qualified research expenses may be eligible for the federal R&D tax credit
under IRC Section 41. Startups can elect to apply up to USD 500,000 of the
credit against payroll taxes.

符合条件的研发支出可依《国内税收法典》第 41 条享受联邦研发税收抵免；
初创企业可选择将至多 50 万美元抵免额用于抵扣工资税。
