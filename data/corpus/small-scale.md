## 免征与减征 / Exemption and Reduction
月销售额未超过 10 万元（按季 30 万元）的小规模纳税人，免征增值税。
小规模纳税人适用 3% 征收率的应税销售收入，减按 1% 征收率征收增值税。

Small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding 100K CNY (300K quarterly) are exempt from VAT.
Taxable sales subject to 3% levy rate are reduced to 1% levy rate.

## 身份与登记 / Status and Registration
年应征增值税销售额 500 万元及以下可登记为小规模纳税人。
达到一定规模应转为一般纳税人。

Annual taxable VAT sales ≤ 5M CNY may register as small-scale taxpayer.
Exceeding the threshold requires conversion to general taxpayer.
